📘 Dataset Description – Ultra-High-Performance Fiber-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC)

This dataset documents the experimental study titled:

“Investigation on the Volume of Steel Fibers in the Behavior of Ultra-High-Performance Fiber-Reinforced Concrete (UHPFRC) Using Sustainable Materials.”

The dataset focuses on how varying volumes of steel fibers influence the mechanical, durability, and microstructural performance of UHPFRC mixes prepared using sustainable supplementary materials.

📦 1. Dataset Overview

This dataset contains:

Mix proportion details for UHPFRC prepared using sustainable cementitious materials.

Mechanical test results for strength performance.

Durability test results for long-term behavior.

Microstructural results from SEM and EDX analysis.

Multiple steel-fiber volume variations (0% to ~3%).

🧱 2. Materials & Mix Information (Table Excluded)

Although the numerical table is not included, the dataset covers the following materials used in UHPFRC:

Binders & Additives

Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC 53 grade)

Silica fume

Alccofine-1203

Ground Granulated Blast Furnace Slag (GGBFS)

Fly ash

Aggregates

River sand

Quartz sand

Chemical Admixtures

Polycarboxylate-based superplasticizer

Fibers

Hooked-end steel fibers (various volume fractions)

The dataset includes multiple mixes where steel fiber volume percentage is varied to study its influence on performance.

🧪 3. Mechanical Properties Dataset

The dataset provides results of the following mechanical tests:

Compressive Strength

Specimen size: 50 × 50 × 50 mm cube

Split Tensile Strength

Specimen size: 75 × 150 mm cylinder

Flexural Strength

Specimen size: 40 × 40 × 160 mm prism

Dataset Includes

Raw test readings

Average values

Effect of increasing steel fiber content on mechanical performance

🛡️ 4. Durability Properties Dataset

The dataset includes durability measurements obtained from the following tests:

Abrasion Resistance Test

Specimen: 20 mm thick × 50 mm breadth × 50 mm diameter

Rapid Chloride Permeability Test (RCPT)

Specimen size: 100 × 50 mm

Water Absorption (Saturated Condition)
Porosity Test

Specimen size: 75 × 150 mm

Alkalinity Test

Based on powdered UHPFRC samples

Dataset Includes

Abrasion mass loss

RCPT charge passed

Water absorption percentage

Porosity values

Alkalinity/pH readings

🔬 5. Microstructural Analysis Dataset

This includes microstructural characterization used to understand the internal structure of UHPFRC:

Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM)

Fiber distribution

Matrix densification

Micro-crack propagation

Energy Dispersive X-Ray Analysis (EDX/EDAX)

Elemental composition of the matrix

Presence of hydration products

📥 6. Use Cases of This Dataset

Researchers can use this dataset for:

Studying the effect of steel fiber volume on UHPC strength & durability

Developing predictive ML/AI models for UHPFRC behavior

Microstructural–mechanical property correlation

Benchmarking sustainable UHPC mix proportions

Reproducible concrete research and validation
